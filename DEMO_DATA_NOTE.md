# Fictional seeded workspace

CareerCollective opens with a fictional candidate profile named **Alex Martin**. The CV, schools, companies and contact address are fictional. Seven application records with Saved, Applied, Interview and Rejected statuses are **simulations**. Their dates and deadlines belong to fictional job listings; nothing was sent to an employer. Seven fictional discussion threads and eleven sample replies show participation, but no shared persistent forum exists.

The CareerCollective application and educational assignment are not an actual recruitment service. Employer feeds visible in Discover are distinct from these local fictional sample records.

Reload the fictional scenario or start blank under **Sample workspace** in the sidebar. The profile is stored as `data/demo_profile.json`, the PDF resume as `data/Alex_Martin_Fictional_CV.pdf`, the application records as `data/demo_applications.csv`, and community replies as `data/demo_community_replies.csv`.
